# Manage Campaign Continuity

Read [the continuity workflow and evidence hierarchy](references/workflow.md) before acting.

Separate objective campaign facts from actor knowledge, rumors, summaries, and Agent inference. Query branch-scoped evidence, identify the smallest contradiction, classify its authority, propose the narrowest repair, and commit a new auditable event/fact/knowledge revision or branch rather than rewriting history silently.

For character recall, request `continuity_query` purpose `actor_memory`: identity, motivational, semantic, and episodic tracks are an audience-safe projection for one authorized actor and branch. Use an explicit query to recover relevant old events beyond the recent window; never infer hidden candidate counts from diagnostics.

Do not disclose private facts while explaining a public inconsistency. Block only for genuinely conflicting indispensable evidence, missing permission, unresolved human canon choice, or stale authority.

Reset context after repair, disclosure, checkout, restore, or any advertised revision recovery.
