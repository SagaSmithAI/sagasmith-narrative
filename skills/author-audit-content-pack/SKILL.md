# Author and Audit Content Packs

Read [the Pack workflow and evidence contract](references/workflow.md) before acting.

Keep all authoring inside MCP-owned drafts. Preserve source evidence, license and distribution facts, dependency locks, candidate decisions, and review findings. Treat finalization as the trust boundary: released content is immutable, and every change becomes a new draft/version.

Distinguish reusable content, runnable modules, and portable campaign seeds. Never present a live campaign backup as a distributable Pack. Do not publish private/OCR/source material merely because a Pack validates.

When a Pack participates in live narrative growth, author a validated `content.runtime_manifest`: exactly one generation-zero root establishes setting and initial Atlas scenes, while every later episode is a new immutable child Pack with explicit parent/root lineage, evidence-bounded basis references, and updated fronts, threads, clues, and character-arc opportunities. Never edit a finalized parent to add an off-Atlas location or emergent scene.

After activation, migration, checkout, or restore, refresh exposure and authoritative context before continuing.
