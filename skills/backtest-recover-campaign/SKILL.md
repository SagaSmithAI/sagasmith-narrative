# Backtest and Recover Campaign

Read [the public-facade regression and recovery contract](references/workflow.md) before acting.

Use a real MCP transport and host behavior. Discover Skills and native tools, record tool-list notifications, context bindings, revisions, idempotency responses, random receipts, settlements, and ending state. Internal service calls, fabricated tool results, or prose claims do not prove a workflow.

Recover by preflight, snapshot/branch inspection, and the smallest authoritative operation. Never weaken evidence, permissions, or transaction guarantees to make a regression green.

After any restart, restore, checkout, phase change, authorization change, or advertised revision recovery, force exposure and context refresh before the next legal native call.
